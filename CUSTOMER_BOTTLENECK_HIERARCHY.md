## Interpretation

- **By breadth:** DOMAIN / GA / GSC / TEAM are tied at 100% — the foundational onboarding
  steps that *every* customer skips.
- **By position:** PROFILE (position 0) is where the *first* customers fall off (3 of 5),
  followed immediately by DOMAIN (position 1, all 5).
- **The binding bottleneck** is the **early onboarding-setup band (PROFILE → DOMAIN →
  analytics → team)** — customers pay, then fail to complete basic setup. Everything
  downstream (execution, value, revenue) is blocked by this.
