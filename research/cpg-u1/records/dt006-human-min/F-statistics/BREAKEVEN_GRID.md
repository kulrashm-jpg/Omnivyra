## How to read it

- `d` = reference disagreement rate (author vs confirmer). `r` = rater disagreement rate.
- Cells are `MARK(valid/fills)`: **V** = no condition triggered; **2** = s15.5(2) INCONCLUSIVE
  (fewer than 90% VALID); **G** = G1 lost (fills < 20, which is also s15.5(1) INCONCLUSIVE);
  **2G** = both.
- The cascade at `analysis.mjs:146-152` is **ordered**, so the losses compose multiplicatively:
  VALID/obs = `(1-d)(1-r)` in regime (i) and `(1-d)` in regime (ii).
- **Regime (i)**: rater disagreements are excluded through a hypothetical new disposition, and so
  spend the same s15.5(2) budget as every other exclusion.
  **Regime (ii)**: rater disagreements are resolved by rule and stay VALID, spending none of it.
  Neither disposition exists in s15.1 today; see `STATISTICAL_CONSEQUENCES.md` s2.
- **E[f]opt** assumes held-out yield equals the pilot yield. **E[f]safe** assumes the SAFETY = 1.5
  contamination correction is actually consumed (held-out yield = y/1.5), which is what SAFETY exists
  for. E[f]safe is the honest baseline and it leaves a **zero** G1 margin at most configurations.
- The G1 column assumes exclusions hit fills in proportion to the fill share of all observations.
  The code implies no distribution; this is the one modelling assumption in the grid.
- **Nothing below weakens a threshold.** Every test is the exact integer comparison from
  `lib/analysis.mjs`, quoted at the top of the script.

## What the grid shows, in one paragraph

s15.5(2) binds before G1 at every configuration except the two capped ones. With a shared exclusion
budget of only 8.70%-10.00% of observations, regime (i) reaches INCONCLUSIVE at roughly
`d + r >= 10%` - so `d = r = 5%` is already fatal at most configurations. Regime (ii) survives
`r = 20%` everywhere, because rater disagreement then spends none of the budget. **The gap between
the two regimes is the single largest design lever.** At T = 1 the run is INCONCLUSIVE at
`d = r = 0` because E[f]opt = 10 < 20; at T = 2 the G1 margin is exactly zero, so the first
excluded fill loses G1.
