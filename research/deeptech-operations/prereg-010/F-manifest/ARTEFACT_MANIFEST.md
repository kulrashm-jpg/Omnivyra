### 1.2 The one gate the verifier cannot protect

`tooling_aggregate_sha256` is **not** a `checkRegistration` blocker — it is ERROR-checked for 64-lowercase-hex
form only. A plausible-looking wrong digest passes silently. It is nonetheless the hardest gate on the whole
registration, because `lib/sampling.mjs:142` binds it into the committed payload whose digest the OP_RETURN
carries, `registered()` and `lib/harness.mjs:44` independently refuse any tree that does not match it, and
`authority_rule` makes a second registration differing in a `SAMPLING_FIELDS` member a **VOID**. So there is
no instrument that both differs from the registered aggregate and can run anything, and no repair path.
This is why the deliberate use of the literal marker
`<<PROVISIONAL: to be supplied by the orchestrator after clean-rebuild verification>>` is correct: the
verifier refuses it loudly with exit 1 and `ERROR tooling_aggregate_sha256 must be 64 lowercase hex
characters`, which fails **louder** than the nine blockers.
