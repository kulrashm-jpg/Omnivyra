# Analytics Interpretation Guide

Key metrics:
- Page views: tracked navigation events.
- Unique visitors: anonymous/session based unique count.
- CTA clicks: elements marked with Omnivera CTA attributes.
- Form starts/submits: plugin and tracker form events.
- Conversion rate: submissions divided by starts or sessions, depending on report.

Signals:
- High traffic low conversion: page has traffic but weak submit rate.
- Strong conversion page: page has meaningful submissions and high conversion rate.
- Weak CTA location: CTA clicks do not lead to submissions.
- Tracking gap: no recent aggregated events.
- Publishing drift: external WordPress post differs from Omnivera state.
