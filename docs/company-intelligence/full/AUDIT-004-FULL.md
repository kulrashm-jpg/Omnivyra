# AUDIT-004 — Company Intelligence Quality & Accuracy Certification (FULL EDITION)

> **Archival Full Edition.** Maintained version: [`../architecture/AUDIT-004.md`](../architecture/AUDIT-004.md). Frozen at ratification. Corrections via [amendments](../amendments/README.md).

**Method:** two targeted read-only passes (prompt-text quality assessment; quality-gate/eval/ground-truth inventory) synthesized with AUDIT-001/002/003. **Classification: Moderate.**

---

## 1. Intelligence Quality Architecture

Quality is produced — and lost — at four planes. **Evidence plane (deterministic):** root + 3 keyword-scored pages, 2,000-char summaries, top-10 sentences per page, deterministic metadata — a hard ceiling on everything downstream. **Generation plane (AI):** 13 LLM call sites at temp 0–0.3 with widely varying evidence discipline, from "return null with source=missing" (extraction) to "suggest plausible completions when unsure" (infer-PT). **Filter plane (deterministic):** one real cliché blocklist (strategy only), classifier override, competitor gates, shape normalization — strong but unevenly applied. **Measurement plane: effectively absent for correctness.** The Phase-1 quality gates (commit bfaa3778) are long-form publishing gates that never touch profile AI (no `companyProfile/*` file imports `phase1QualityGates`; the gate operates on `{title, contentHtml}`). The capability-validation framework's "hallucination check" is a non-empty-payload check, and most profile-adjacent capabilities have grounding/hallucination checks **disabled**. Shadow mode measures canonical-vs-legacy divergence, not correctness. The only factual-quality judging (an LLM-judge with `factualCorrectness`/`hallucination` dimensions) is offline lab scaffolding that does not import production prompt code. **No production ground-truth or correction-rate loop exists** — `company_profile_refinements` and `company_context_review_events` persist the raw material for one but nothing analyzes it.

## 2. Evidence Coverage Matrix

Certified: the system's best evidence is systematically unavailable to most of its generators. Crawl summaries → extraction + strategy draft only. Structured metadata → not injected as extraction fields. JSON-LD → fingerprinting only (parsed then discarded as hashes). Existing profile → passed to extraction as `_currentProfile`, unused. Historical content (blogs/scheduled posts) → the strategy draft only (the richest voice/messaging evidence, used by exactly one workflow). Intelligence context → define-context-intelligence, MarketPulse, recommendations. Knowledge graph → define-target-customer only. External knowledge (Wikidata/Wikipedia) → suggest-competitors + facts-lookup only. BFS crawl (up to 250 pages) → context enrichment only. **Four high-value grounding assets (KG, Wikidata/Wikipedia understanding, the blog/post corpus, the BFS crawl) each serve exactly one workflow.**

## 3. Field Quality Certification

Per-field accuracy classification with the deciding evidence: **Excellent** — website_url (verified). **High** — name, logo/favicon/country/socials, company facts (Wikidata + human review), engine-path competitors (the only AI-adjacent subsystem with contract tests locking its quality gates), strategy fields (richest grounding of any AI workflow *and* the only workflow with a semantic output filter). **Medium** — products_services, target_audience (inference-heavy), unique_value (broken confidence key), industry/category (correct when signals strong, silently generic when weak), commercial 7 (depends entirely on user engagement quality; no server-side validation on save). **Low** — brand_voice (weakest signal websites emit), goals (websites rarely state goals — near-zero direct evidence), marketing intelligence 7 (derivative: AI summarizing prior AI output, no cliché filter, no save validation), campaign_purpose (unguarded loop, generic-emotion-word prone), problem/transformation 9 (plausible-sounding, weakly grounded; the deterministic fallback output is **Very Low**).

## 4. Prompt Quality Certification

Strong prompts: `buildStrategyDerivationPrompt` (evidence rules + "return fewer items rather than generic filler" + the only cliché blocklist `containsMeaningfulSignal`), `generateMissingFieldQuestions` (allow/block token filter enforced both sides of the LLM), `suggest-competitors` (hard exclusion list, "omit any name or domain you are unsure is real"), `define-context-intelligence` (temp 0, labeled answers, deterministic reconstruction safety net), `buildExtractionPrompt` (self-labeling source/confidence, null-on-missing). Weak prompts, ranked by generic/hallucination likelihood: (1) infer-PT — internally contradictory ("Use ONLY concepts grounded in the profile" vs. "add pain_symptoms typical for that industry" + "suggest plausible completions when unsure"), no post-filter; (2) define-problem-transformation — "MUST propose at least one concrete field improvement each turn" forces output without evidence, and `deterministicRefineFallback` injects hardcoded company-agnostic strings ("From scattered, reactive decision-making to clear, confident, and prioritized action"); (3) PT fill mode ("infer best deterministic assumption"); (4) the marketing intelligence draft (soft "prefer specific" only, no output filter, full-profile dump); (5) define-campaign-purpose (no anti-invention rule, model-driven loop, generic seed examples). **Certified pattern: anti-hallucination discipline is inversely correlated with field abstraction** — concrete fields get the strict prompts; the abstract fields, where hallucination is hardest to detect, get the permissive ones.

## 5. Grounding Quality Certification

Completeness: no workflow receives complete grounding — even the best (strategy) lacks Wikidata/KG; the best-grounded chat (define-target-customer) lacks crawl evidence. Missing: the MI draft and five of six chats operate with no primary evidence (AI grounded in prior AI). Duplicate: five parallel serializations of the same profile, each choosing different fields. Inconsistent: dedup grounding (KG) in 1 of 6 chats; the marketing chat passes only 3 of 7 commercial fields. Stale: a 7-day staleness gate + 1/3/7-day tier cooldowns + no scheduled crawl; all chat grounding reads with `autoRefine:false`, so conversations can be grounded in weeks-old extraction. **Better grounding exists but is unused:** the KG (built for exactly this, 1/6 adoption), `buildCompanyUnderstanding`'s Wikidata/Wikipedia block (competitors only), the blog/post corpus (strategy only), the 250-page BFS crawl (enrichment only), and JSON-LD product/service types (fingerprint only).

## 6. Intelligence Consistency Certification

Certified: the same company can produce divergent intelligence across workflows, and no mechanism would detect it. Strategy and marketing drafts run in a single `Promise.all` on the same input, never seeing each other; reconciliation is a fill-if-empty union, not a consistency check — `differentiation` and `competitive_advantages` can contradict. Industry consistency relies on a single advisory conflict flag that blocks nothing; the persisted industry prefers the user baseline while the classifier writes classification independently. Audience↔brand-voice, PT↔messaging, competitors↔positioning: **zero cross-field enforcement**; the only cross-field mechanism (competitor-derived differentiation nudges) fires solely for audience-led archetypes with non-empty competitor intel — business-first companies get none. Cross-chat consistency exists only where the KG is wired (1 endpoint). Confidence consistency is broken by construction (4 key/vocabulary mismatches).

## 7. Deterministic Quality Certification

Net-positive — the system's main defense. Improves quality: the refresh gate/change-detection stack; `containsMeaningfulSignal` (the single most effective quality mechanism; also rejects any item under 3 words); the generic-industry stripper; the creator-contamination guard (discards the whole contaminated draft); the competitor gates + revalidation. Dual-edged: the classifier override of AI industry (improves vs AI hallucination; reduces vs user truth — overrides typed user values and degrades to generic `'business_operations'` on weak signals). **Reduces quality (three certified cases):** the PT deterministic fallback (the one case of deterministic logic actively writing generic company-agnostic content into intelligence fields); monotonic confidence (`Math.max` — the quality signal can never reflect degradation); the classifier-vs-user override. Also: schema-cache column-drop retry (silent data loss on unapplied migrations).

## 8. Intelligence Loss Register

Where useful information disappears, in pipeline order: **crawl** (JS-rendered content invisible; pages beyond 4 never read; `headings` extracted as an always-empty array; 2,000-char truncation; only sentences >40 chars scoring on 17 keywords survive); **structured data** (JSON-LD product/service/organization types parsed then used only as hashes); **cleaning** (an LLM deletes evidence before extraction — unrecoverable, unlogged); **prompt construction** (≤40 sources cap; existing profile discarded by extraction; metadata not injected); **serialization** (each chat sees a different hand-picked subset; 4 of 7 commercial fields never cross into the marketing chat); **normalization** (list caps 0–5/0–3, 28-word worldview cap, empty-array→null, <3-word rejection); **confidence** (bands written under wrong keys are permanently unreadable; `'Needs Review'` collapses to low; PT quality flattened to uniform 'Medium'); **persistence** (silent column dropping; `report_settings` full-replace can erase sibling stores' data); **downstream** (the refinement audit trail and enrichment review events are persisted but never analyzed — **the system's only ground-truth signal is collected and discarded**).

## 9. Consumer Quality Matrix

Content generation (whole profile via `buildContentContext`) is the highest-volume amplifier of the weakest fields (brand_voice, goals, key_messages, content_themes) — generic voice/messaging propagates into every article/post. Campaigns/planner and BOLT build campaign framing on the two least-validated field groups (campaign purpose, PT). Recommendations mix High strategy inputs with Low MI. Reports draw on the high-quality deterministic fields (least exposed). MarketPulse is comparatively well-fed (competitor details High + review-gated context intelligence). Customer-success/activation key decisions to `overall_confidence` ≥60 — a metric that can't decrease and miscounts field trust. **Strongest upstream fields:** website, identity, logo/socials, company facts, engine-path competitors, strategy fields. **Weakest:** marketing intelligence, PT, goals, brand voice, campaign purpose — precisely the fields content generation leans on hardest.

## 11. Final Quality Certification & Classification

**Moderate.** Bimodal by field tier. Highly reliable: website/identity resolution, deterministic crawl-derived facts, Wikidata-backed company facts, engine-path competitors (contract-tested), and strategy fields (rich multi-source grounding + a semantic output filter). Weak: the entire marketing-intelligence tier (profile-only grounding, no cliché filter, no save validation), problem/transformation (invention-licensed prompts plus a deterministic fallback that fabricates identical boilerplate for every company), goals and brand voice (fields the evidence base structurally cannot support), and campaign purpose (unguarded model-driven loop). **Three structural causes, certified:** (1) evidence starvation with abundance nearby — the generators that most need grounding receive none, while four rich grounding assets sit one import away, each serving a single workflow; (2) the quality machinery is real but misallocated — the cliché blocklist exists on one workflow, the dedup grounding on one endpoint, the contract tests on competitors/confidence, the publishing gates on blogs; the fields with the highest hallucination surface have the least of all four; (3) nothing measures correctness — shadow measures divergence, adoption measures migration, context quality measures completeness/freshness, and the LLM-judge that measures factual correctness is offline; meanwhile the ground-truth signal the system already collects (refinement diffs, human accept/reject, user locks) is persisted and never read, so the system cannot currently know whether its intelligence is right. Not "Weak" because the deterministic backbone (8/10), the reliable-field tier, the historical-failure guards (each encoding a caught-and-fixed incident), and the strategy workflow demonstrate the platform can produce high-quality intelligence and knows how to defend it. Not "Good" because the weak tier is not an edge case — it is the marketing/PT/purpose layer that the highest-volume consumer (content generation) depends on most, the chat-save path launders unvalidated LLM output into locked user truth, and no production mechanism can detect any of it happening.
